If you liked Prodigy Math Game, you'll love Out of Asia! (and the other versions, too!)

Out of Asia! is a game that is based on the Prodigy Math Game, but it has a different story and different characters. The game is set in the USA, and you have to escape from the USA to get back to Asia.
